**1. Drop-in Stewardship Volunteering with Friends of Acadia** — *Regular Event*
- **What:** Join volunteer crew leaders for trail stewardship — cutting back vegetation, clearing drainage features, building bogwalk, tread work, weeding the carriage roads, and other tasks.
- **When:** Every Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday from June through end of October. Remaining dates in our window: **Oct 20, 22, 24, 27, 29, 31**.
- **Where:** Park and meet at the **Hulls Cove Storage Facility**, near the Hulls Cove Visitor Center, Visitor Center Rd, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. Enter via the Hulls Cove Visitor Center entrance off Route 3, continue past the Visitor Center parking lot on Paradise Hill Road; when the road splits, bear left to the Hulls Cove Storage Facility. There is an "authorized vehicles only" and "do not enter" sign — volunteers should continue past; signs will be out to guide you.
- **Notes:** Projects run 8:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m., weather permitting. No experience or reservation required.
- **Coordinates:** 44.409348, -68.249377
- **Tags:** friends of acadia, volunteer, stewardship

**2. Wetland Revival – Walking the Path of Restoration** — *Regular Event*
- **What:** Join a ranger on a mostly level **2.3-mile walk through the Great Meadow**, learning how Acadia National Park, the Schoodic Institute, and Friends of Acadia are working together to restore a damaged wetland.
- **When:** **Oct 21, 23, 28, 30** (the feed lists "All day" — check with the park for exact start time).
- **Where:** **Outside Sieur de Monts Nature Center**.
- **Notes:** Mostly level 2.3 miles — good short-day option for the crew.
- **Coordinates:** 44.362095, -68.207892
- **Accessibility:** Sign language, live audio description, assistive listening devices, or other accommodation can be requested at least 10 days in advance at 207-288-3338, option 0, or acadia_information@nps.gov (go.nps.gov/AcadiaAccessibility).

**3. EeeK of Ecology Halloween Festival for Children** — *Special Event*

**Plain-language summary of closures affecting the crew:**
- **Beech Mountain Loop (east section):** closed below the summit/fire tower. Use the outer loop, Beech South Ridge, or Beech West Ridge trails instead.
- **Schoodic Mountain Road:** closed from Schoodic Loop Road to Schoodic Head summit. If we do Schoodic, plan on hiking the loop road route rather than driving to the summit.
- **Ocean Path (south section):** closed from the trailhead across from Otter Point parking to the southern terminus. **This one matters — Ocean Path is one of Acadia's signature hikes (Sand Beach → Thunder Hole → Otter Cliff). The lower/southern portion near Otter Point is off-limits; the Sand Beach/Thunder Hole end remains accessible.** Note that the park's Current Conditions page also notes storm damage history and temporary repairs at Ocean Path from the January 2024 storms; check current status before committing to a full-length route.
- No alert-level closures listed for the Park Loop Road itself, the carriage roads, or the main visitor facilities as of this digest.

### Planning notes from the park home page (nps.gov/acad)
- **Entrance pass required year-round.** Vehicles must display a pass clearly visible through the windshield. Buy and print a pass online in advance.
- **Vehicle reservations** are required to drive Cadillac Summit Road from late May to mid-October. Reservations are **sold online only, not at the park**. Note: if our trip lands after mid-October, this requirement ends for the season.
- **Camping takes planning:** all campsite reservations must be made online in advance. There is **no backcountry camping and no overnight parking** allowed in Acadia.
- **Off-season is a real thing:** the park actively promotes November–April as "Get the Most Out of An Off-Season Visit" — there's a lot to do in the off-season. Good news if our trip slips later in the window.
- **Weather:** be prepared for quick changes from warm and sunny to cold and rainy; mountain weather on Cadillac and other peaks can be drastically different from Bar Harbor. Check the Weather page for details.
- **Wabanaki Nations:** resources to learn more about the heritage, culture, and continuing presence of the tribes known collectively as Wabanaki, "People of the Dawnland."
- **Smart stuff to know:** Bark Ranger/pet privileges, park rules & regulations, permits for special events/research/climbing, invasive species, leave your firewood at home (don't transport firewood).

### Other conditions-page notes
- **Beaches:** Sand Beach, Echo Lake Beach, and Lake Wood are monitored weekly for bacteria **June through August only** — monitoring has ended for the season.
- **Roads/parking/trails detailed lists:** the Current Conditions page loads facility-specific lists via scripts (rendered in the page as "Loading results…"), so the page itself is best viewed in a browser; the **Operating Hours & Seasons** page (nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/hours.htm) is where the park publishes detailed facility opening/closing dates — check it before finalizing dates.
- **Winter storms (Jan 2024) damage history:** significant storm damage occurred across the park (Ocean Path temporary repairs, Sand Beach staircase damage, Little Hunters Beach steps washed out, Otter Cove causeway riprap damage, Seawall Picnic Area and Campground downed trees, Islesford Historical Museum area damage, Rockefeller Hall roof damage). Crews continue to respond to long-term impacts — **exercise caution while exploring**.
- **Park contact:** PO Box 177, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 · 207-288-3338

## Quick Reference for the Crew

- **Buy the entrance pass online before arrival** — pass must be visible through the windshield, year-round.
- **Cadillac Summit Road vehicle reservations end mid-October** — if we arrive after that, no reservation needed (but confirm the park's current cut-off).
- **Campground reservations are online-only, in advance** — no backcountry camping, no overnight parking.
- **Trail closures to plan around:** Beech Mountain Loop east section, Schoodic Mountain Road, Ocean Path southern section (from Otter Point trailhead to southern terminus).
- **Looking for ranger-led options:** the ranger-led Great Meadow wetland walk (Oct 21, 23, 28, 30) and the volunteer stewardship sessions (Tue/Thu/Sat through October) both offer guided time in the park.
- **Kick-around options:** Great Meadow walk (2.3 mi, mostly level); carriage roads for easy miles; Beech Mountain via an alternate route; Park Loop Road area highlights (Sand Beach, Thunder Hole — keeping clear of the closed southern Ocean Path section).
- **Weather planning:** expect quick swings; mountain conditions differ sharply from Bar Harbor.
- **Park phone:** 207-288-3338 (main line, also for accessibility requests, option 0)
